**hirly** is an AI job search for individual job seekers. It scores open roles against your resume, writes a resume and cover letter for the job you pick, and fills the employer's application form — you press submit, or hirly submits a job you chose, with your consent for that job.

- **Website** — [hirly.me](https://hirly.me/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=org-profile)
- **Job board** — [hirly.me/jobs](https://hirly.me/jobs?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=org-profile)
- **hirly in your AI assistant** — [hirly.me/ai-assistants](https://hirly.me/ai-assistants?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=org-profile)
- **MCP server** — `https://mcp.hirly.me/mcp` · [setup and tool list](https://github.com/Hirly-Me/hirly-mcp)
- **Founder on X** — [@thakur_g](https://x.com/thakur_g)

## Job lists

Open jobs from company career pages hirly reads directly, newest first. Each list is a repository whose README is a table: company, role, location, date, and a link to the job.
